01 / WEB PROJECTProblemSet Practice with a purpose.
THE STARTING POINT

I wanted a website where each question mattered and unlocked a new concept.

THE IDEA

Make practice feel intentional: a curated path through mathematics, organized by topic, with the next problem in focus.

WHAT IT BECAME

ProblemSet, a place to spend less time searching and more time solving.

Visit ProblemSet ↗
02 / DESKTOP APPProblemSlate Space to think.
THE STARTING POINT

I wanted a quiet place to work through problems without the clutter of a full drawing app.

THE IDEA

Keep the canvas and the tools simple enough to disappear when I’m working through an idea.

WHAT IT BECAME

ProblemSlate, a focused whiteboard for writing and sketching on macOS and Windows.

Visit ProblemSlate ↗
03 / MATHEMATICAL WRITINGMy Papers Ideas worth keeping.
THE STARTING POINT

I wanted a simple home for my mathematical writing, where every paper is a click away.

THE IDEA

Let the writing speak for itself: a neat, easy-to-browse home for papers and notes.

WHAT IT BECAME

One place to share the mathematics I’ve been exploring, with each PDF easy to open.
